DEC.12

Hexagram 19 – Lin: Steady Approach

Upper: Earth (☷)  ·  Lower: Lake (☱)

Core Meaning

A period of growth is approaching, so this is a good time to prepare and act. Opportunity matters most when you are ready for it; strengthen the foundation now so you can handle what arrives.

“I move toward growth with steady steps and a solid foundation.”

Blessing

May you receive steady support and offer the same care to the people growing beside you.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship is moving closer, and both of you may sense the next step. Express your intentions honestly and notice whether the other person is equally ready so progress remains mutual.

Express your feelings and future plans clearly:

  • Notice whether the other person is ready for the next step.
  • Back your words with consistent action.

Career

An important opportunity may be approaching through a promotion, project, or partnership. Prepare now by finishing current work well and closing any skill gaps.

Do your current work well so you are ready when opportunity appears:

  • Identify the skills and resources you still need.
  • Ask for opportunities instead of waiting for them.

Health

This is a good time to act on a health plan you have been considering. Set one specific goal and use the current motivation to build a lasting habit.

Start the health plan you have been postponing:

  • Choose one specific short-term goal.
  • Use current motivation to establish a repeatable habit.

Finances

A new source of income or investment opportunity may be approaching. Review your cash position and knowledge first so you can judge it calmly when it appears.

Review your cash position and financial knowledge in advance:

  • Research before acting on a new opportunity.
  • Decide how new income would support your goals.

Relationships

New friendships or partnerships may be ready to deepen. Approach people you respect, be sincere, and give new connections room to develop naturally.

Approach people you genuinely respect:

  • Let natural meetings develop into real connection.
  • Give new relationships time before defining them.
