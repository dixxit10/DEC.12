Hexagram 39 – Jian: Stop at the Obstacle

Upper: Water (☵)  ·  Lower: Mountain (☶)

Core Meaning

The way ahead is blocked. Pushing straight through will only deepen the problem. Pause, reassess, seek good advice, and choose a safer route. Slowing down is not giving up.

“I slow down before obstacles and choose the steadier path.”

Blessing

May difficulty help you pause, look inward, and find the path that truly works.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship faces real obstacles. Do not force progress or demand answers. Stabilize what you have and step back if needed until the pressure clears.

Stabilize the relationship first:

  • Step back when pressure is high.
  • Decide again when the situation is clearer.

Career

A project or path is blocked. Pause, assess resources, ask experienced people, and change routes if necessary.

Pause and assess the real obstacle:

  • Ask experienced people for input.
  • Change routes if the cost of pushing is too high.

Health

Recovery may be slower or uneven. Avoid constant changes; slow down, follow qualified advice, and give the process time.

Slow down and follow qualified advice:

  • Give one plan enough time.
  • Treat recovery as a process.

Finances

Cash flow or investments may be under pressure. Protect liquidity, reduce risk, and rebuild the plan before chasing a quick fix.

Protect cash flow and reduce risk:

  • Rework the plan with sound advice.
  • Choose stability before speed.

Relationships

Communication may feel blocked. Change your approach first, and use a trusted mediator if direct pressure is making things worse.

Adjust your approach before pushing harder:

  • Ask a trusted person for help if needed.
  • An indirect route can work better.
