Hexagram 28 – Daguo: Bearing the Load

Upper: Lake (☱)  ·  Lower: Wind (☴)

Core Meaning

The load is heavier than usual, and ordinary methods may not be enough. This calls for courage and practical support. Carry what matters, but do not confuse responsibility with doing everything alone.

“I meet heavy responsibility with courage, judgment, and support.”

Blessing

May you be supported while carrying what matters, so responsibility does not become isolation.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship may be under unusual pressure. Say when the load is too much and share responsibility instead of silently carrying it alone.

Say when the pressure is too much:

  • Share the load.
  • Express stress before it turns into resentment.

Career

You may be facing a high-stakes task, tight deadline, or unusually large responsibility. Use the team and available resources instead of absorbing every burden yourself.

Use the team and available resources:

  • Use different methods when normal ones are not enough.
  • Handle the most critical issue first.

Health

Heavy stress requires more attention to recovery, not less. Protect sleep, notice warning signs, and seek support when the load is affecting your health.

Protect sleep and recovery under stress:

  • Ask for support.
  • Take warning signs seriously.

Finances

Large expenses or debt may be creating pressure. Rework cash flow, cut what can be cut, and seek qualified help when the numbers are too tight to manage alone.

Rework cash flow and reduce pressure:

  • Seek professional financial help if needed.
  • Stabilize first, then plan long term.

Relationships

You may be supporting other people right now, but you also need support. Ask trusted people for help before your own capacity is exhausted.

Remember that you also need support:

  • Ask trusted people for help.
  • Care for yourself while caring for others.
