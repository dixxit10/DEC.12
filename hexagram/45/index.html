Hexagram 45 – Cui: Gathering Together

Upper: Lake (☱)  ·  Lower: Earth (☷)

Core Meaning

This is a time of gathering. People, resources, and shared purpose can come together more powerfully than any solo effort. Bring the right people together with sincerity and a clear goal, and the group can accomplish more.

“I bring people and purpose together to create something greater.”

Blessing

May the right people gather around you and build something meaningful together.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Your lives are drawing closer. Invite each other into important moments and let shared experiences deepen the bond.

Invite them into your world:

  • Share important moments.
  • Build closeness through time together.

Career

People and resources are gathering around you. Unite the right team around a clear goal and use that momentum well.

Gather the right people:

  • Set one clear goal.
  • Give everyone a defined role.

Health

You do not need to handle health alone. Build support through professionals, family, or a reliable community.

Build a reliable support system:

  • Accept professional help.
  • Do not face everything alone.

Finances

Connections and shared resources may create financial opportunities. Work only with trusted people and clear rules.

Use trusted partnerships:

  • Agree on goals and rules.
  • Keep shared money clearly directed.

Relationships

Your social circle is active and expanding. Treat each connection sincerely and build a real sense of belonging.

Value each gathering:

  • Build genuine community.
  • Let strong connections support you.
