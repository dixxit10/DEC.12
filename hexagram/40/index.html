Hexagram 40 – Jie: Release After the Storm

Upper: Thunder (☳)  ·  Lower: Water (☵)

Core Meaning

Pressure is easing and a difficult situation is beginning to open. Release what no longer needs to be carried, soften rigid positions, and deal with old issues while conditions are improving.

“I release what has weighed me down and make room for a lighter beginning.”

Blessing

May what has held you for so long begin to loosen and resolve.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Tension is easing and misunderstandings can be cleared. Take a sincere first step toward repair and stop carrying old injuries into the next conversation.

Take the first step toward repair:

  • Clear up misunderstandings while the door is open.
  • Leave old hurt behind.

Career

A bottleneck is loosening. Use the opening to resolve delayed issues and remove processes that no longer serve you.

Resolve delayed issues while conditions improve:

  • Remove outdated burdens.
  • Use the opening to reset.

Health

Tension and discomfort may be easing. Reduce unnecessary stress and give your body real recovery time.

Make room for real rest:

  • Reduce sources of tension.
  • Let recovery finish at an easier pace.

Finances

Financial pressure may be loosening. Settle overdue matters, collect what is owed, and restore healthy cash flow.

Settle delayed financial issues:

  • Restore cash flow.
  • Review the plan once pressure eases.

Relationships

Old misunderstandings can finally be discussed. Reach out, release old assumptions, and let the relationship move again.

Reach out with a willingness to reconcile:

  • Release old assumptions.
  • Let the relationship move again.
