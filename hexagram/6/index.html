Hexagram 6 – Song: End the Conflict

Upper: Heaven (☰)  ·  Lower: Water (☵)

Core Meaning

You are in a tense situation where each side believes it is right. Proving who is right may cost more than it solves. Step back, clarify the real issue, and use a neutral third party when needed.

“I let go of the need to prove myself and resolve conflict with calm judgment.”

Blessing

May you leave unnecessary arguments behind and save your energy for what truly matters.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Both sides may feel justified. Stop trying to win long enough to hear what each person actually cares about.

Decide whether the issue is worth continuing to fight over:

  • Discuss one core problem at a time.
  • Pause when the conversation starts repeating.

Career

Disputes over roles, interests, or ideas need clear facts and boundaries. Look for the smallest workable agreement before escalating.

Turn verbal disagreements into clear facts and records:

  • Find the smallest point both sides can accept.
  • Try neutral mediation before the conflict escalates.

Health

Conflicting health advice can be confusing. Compare reliable evidence and ask qualified professionals about the points that disagree.

Compare conflicting advice against reliable evidence:

  • Ask qualified professionals about contradictions.
  • Avoid changing many treatments at once.

Finances

Money disputes need records, not memory. Put terms in writing and reconcile the numbers before the disagreement grows.

Keep written records of payments, loans, and agreements:

  • Pause new transactions involving disputed amounts.
  • Seek professional advice for major disputes.

Relationships

Not every argument needs a winner. Create space, let emotions settle, and return to the issue when the conversation can be useful.

End circular arguments when they stop being productive:

  • Speak directly with the person involved.
  • Do not recruit others to take sides.
