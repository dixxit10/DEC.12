DEC.12

Hexagram 29 – Kan: Steady Through Danger

Upper: Water (☵)  ·  Lower: Water (☵)

Core Meaning

You are facing repeated challenges, one after another. Like water, keep moving without losing your center. Stay calm, act with integrity, and take each difficulty one step at a time.

“I move through difficulty with a steady heart and keep my inner light alive.”

Blessing

May you remain calm and grounded even through repeated challenges.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship is under pressure from misunderstandings or outside stress. Avoid making decisions while emotional; steady yourself and show care through consistent actions.

Do not decide while emotional:

  • Show care through steady actions.
  • Learn from each setback.

Career

Work may bring one challenge after another. Break large problems into smaller ones and solve them calmly instead of forcing a quick result.

Break large problems into smaller steps:

  • Respond calmly, not forcefully.
  • Build skill through each challenge.

Health

Symptoms or setbacks may come and go. Follow sound professional guidance, keep a steady routine, and do not panic over short-term changes.

Follow professional guidance:

  • Do not panic over short-term changes.
  • Keep a steady recovery routine.

Finances

Finances may face repeated expenses or losses. Protect your cash floor, stop adding risk, and repair the situation step by step.

Protect essential cash flow:

  • Do not take bigger risks to recover losses.
  • Repair gaps step by step.

Relationships

You may feel misunderstood or isolated. Stay consistent and honest instead of trying to win everyone over; reliable supporters will remain.

Act with consistent integrity:

  • Do not try to please everyone.
  • Value the people who truly support you.
