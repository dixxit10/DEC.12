Hexagram 21 – Shike: Clear Judgment

Upper: Fire (☲)  ·  Lower: Thunder (☳)

Core Meaning

This is a time for clear judgment and decisive action. The obstacle in front of you can be handled once you see the real issue. Act when action is needed, and let go when it is time to move on.

“I see the issue clearly and act with firm judgment.”

Blessing

May you have the courage to clear what blocks your path and create space for clarity.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

A long-standing issue may be holding the relationship back. Bring it into the open with honesty and care, then work on one practical solution together.

Name the core issue honestly without attacking:

  • Handle one problem at a time.
  • Set needed boundaries calmly and firmly.

Career

A clear obstacle can no longer be ignored. Identify the real cause and deal with it directly instead of working around it.

Face the hardest obstacle directly:

  • Understand the root cause before choosing a response.
  • Once you decide, act.

Health

Your body may be giving a clear warning. Do not delay; address the symptom, get appropriate evaluation, and change what needs to change.

Take clear warning signs seriously:

  • Make needed changes without delay.
  • Seek qualified professional advice when needed.

Finances

A firm financial decision may be needed, such as cutting a loss or changing an arrangement. Face the numbers and act before the cost grows.

Cut losses or adjust when the facts support it:

  • Do not add more out of frustration.
  • Set a clear deadline for decisions.

Relationships

A boundary or unresolved frustration may need to be stated clearly. Speak early, calmly, and firmly instead of waiting until resentment builds.

State boundaries before resentment builds:

  • Be firm without attacking.
  • After speaking clearly, leave room to reset.
