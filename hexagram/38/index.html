Hexagram 38 – Kui: Common Ground

Upper: Fire (☲)  ·  Lower: Lake (☱)

Core Meaning

Differences in views or values are clear now. Do not force agreement. Give small differences room, and seek common ground on what truly matters; contrast can become a strength.

“I make room for differences while looking for common ground.”

Blessing

May you stay understanding when views differ and find a distance that feels right for both sides.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Differences in personality or values do not have to damage the relationship. Stop trying to reshape each other and find a way of relating that works for both.

Accept differences without forcing change:

  • Find a comfortable way to relate.
  • Let differences add depth, not damage.

Career

Disagreement is present. Identify the shared objective first, then keep the remaining differences within a workable range.

Find the shared direction first:

  • Keep disagreements discussable.
  • Use complementary strengths.

Health

Conflicting signals or advice require caution. Watch your actual response and seek coordinated professional guidance when needed.

Follow your body's real response:

  • Avoid conflicting plans at the same time.
  • Get coordinated professional advice if needed.

Finances

Financial partners may disagree on direction. Compare the tradeoffs openly and look for an option that protects both sides.

Lay out the pros and cons calmly:

  • Seek a solution that serves both sides.
  • Allow time before major decisions.

Relationships

Not everyone will fit your values or lifestyle. Respect the difference, keep a friendly distance, and invest in people who truly fit.

Respect differences:

  • Keep a friendly distance where needed.
  • Invest in people who truly fit.
