Hexagram 32 – Heng: Lasting Commitment

Upper: Thunder (☳)  ·  Lower: Wind (☴)

Core Meaning

Lasting results come from steady effort. Choose what is worth maintaining and keep working at it with a sustainable rhythm. Stay true to the core while adjusting your methods when needed.

“I stay committed to what matters and let consistency work over time.”

Blessing

May the relationships and values you cherish grow steadier and deeper with time.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Love lasts through consistent care more than occasional intensity. Put commitment into small daily actions and value the stability you have built.

Put commitment into daily actions:

  • Value steady companionship.
  • Show up when it matters.

Career

Your work needs time to mature. Stay with a worthwhile direction and keep building skill and credibility instead of changing course too often.

Stay with a worthwhile direction:

  • Keep building skill and trust.
  • Do not overreact to short-term results.

Health

Health improves through habits you can sustain. If you slip, restart instead of giving up; consistency matters more than perfection.

Choose habits you can sustain:

  • Restart after setbacks.
  • Measure progress over months.

Finances

Wealth grows through steady contributions and a long-term plan. Avoid changing strategy because of short-term market moves.

Follow a consistent long-term plan:

  • Invest regularly.
  • Let time work for you.

Relationships

Strong friendships are built through time. Stay in touch, keep promises, and show up when people need you.

Invest in relationships worth keeping:

  • Stay in touch and keep promises.
  • Be present when needed.
