Hexagram 30 – Li: Steady Light

Upper: Fire (☲)  ·  Lower: Fire (☲)

Core Meaning

Light needs something sound to rest on. Choose people, systems, and values that help you stay clear and grounded. Keep strengthening your own foundation so your light can last.

“I follow what is right and let my light support both myself and others.”

Blessing

May you stay aligned with the right direction and let your light remain steady.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

A healthy relationship allows closeness without losing yourself. Stay near people who help you grow, while keeping your own center.

Stay close to people who help you grow:

  • Keep your own center.
  • Support each other.

Career

The right team, mentor, or platform can help your skills stand out. Keep learning so you become a source of strength, not only a recipient of support.

Choose the right platform and partners:

  • Keep building your own skills.
  • Grow into a source of strength.

Health

Your body benefits from stable anchors such as regular routines, trusted care, or a supportive partner. Consistency makes progress easier to sustain.

Build stable routines and support:

  • Use trusted professional care.
  • Let consistency anchor your health.

Finances

Favor sound investments and reliable advice, but keep your own judgment. Learn continuously rather than following tips blindly.

Follow sound long-term principles:

  • Keep independent judgment.
  • Avoid chasing short-term tips.

Relationships

Spend more time with groups and mentors who bring out your best. As you grow, pass that support forward to others.

Join groups that help you grow:

  • Learn from good mentors.
  • Support others in return.
