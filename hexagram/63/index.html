DEC.12

Hexagram 63 – Jiji: Completion in Balance

Upper: Water (☵)  ·  Lower: Fire (☲)

Core Meaning

A major stage has been completed and order is in place. This is a welcome period of stability, but completion also begins the next cycle. Stay alert, protect what is working, and notice small changes before they grow.

“I protect what has been completed and stay alert even in success.”

Blessing

May you remain humble and attentive so that what is good can last.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship is stable after a period of adjustment. Appreciate that stability, but keep bringing care and freshness into it.

Protect the stability you built:

  • Keep the relationship fresh.
  • Do not take harmony for granted.

Career

A goal or project has reached completion. Celebrate, then check for small weaknesses so the result can hold.

Celebrate, then review:

  • Fix small weaknesses early.
  • Protect the completed work.

Health

Your condition is relatively stable. Keep the habits that are working and respond early to small changes.

Keep effective habits:

  • Notice small changes.
  • Adjust early.

Finances

A financial milestone may have been reached. Protect the gains, keep risk controlled, and prepare for the next stage.

Protect gains:

  • Watch for turning points.
  • Prepare for the next stage.

Relationships

Relationships are running smoothly. Keep maintaining them instead of assuming harmony will take care of itself.

Keep maintaining harmony:

  • Repair small cracks early.
  • Do not become careless.
