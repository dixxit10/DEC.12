Hexagram 17 – Sui: Flow with Change

Upper: Lake (☱)  ·  Lower: Thunder (☳)

Core Meaning

Change is the constant right now. Instead of resisting it, adjust with the situation while keeping your own principles. Following the flow does not mean following blindly; stay flexible without losing your center.

“I move with change and find my rhythm through flexibility.”

Blessing

May you move with life instead of forcing it, and find unexpected joy along the way.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

The relationship is adjusting as your needs or routines change. Be willing to meet each other halfway, but do not lose yourself; communicate what matters with calm firmness.

Match the other person's pace and observe how the relationship changes:

  • Discuss important needs calmly and clearly.
  • Recheck what matters most to both of you.

Career

Your environment or team direction may be changing, so old methods may no longer fit. Adapt to the new direction while keeping your standards and professional judgment.

Adjust to the new direction instead of clinging to the old plan:

  • Keep your standards while adapting.
  • Use change to reallocate time and resources.

Health

Your body may shift with changes in daily life. Adjust your routine as needed, but keep basic anchors such as sleep and regular meals.

Adjust your pace to what your body needs now:

  • Protect sleep and meal routines when your schedule changes.
  • Do not switch methods after every small fluctuation.

Finances

Financial conditions may be changing. Review your allocation and adapt when needed, but do not chase every trend or abandon your investment rules.

Review your allocation instead of clinging to the old plan:

  • Adapt without chasing every market move.
  • Keep more cash available during uncertain periods.

Relationships

Your social circle may be changing. Stay open to new interactions, but keep your boundaries and do not force yourself to fit in.

Stay present and let go of fixed assumptions:

  • Do not force yourself to fit in.
  • Stay open to connections that arise naturally.
