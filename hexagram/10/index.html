Hexagram 10 – Lu: Treading Carefully

Upper: Heaven (☰)  ·  Lower: Lake (☱)

Core Meaning

You are moving through a situation that looks ordinary but carries real risk. Respect limits, follow the rules, and act with care. Good judgment can make a difficult path safe.

“I move with care and respect, staying steady through risk.”

Blessing

May each careful step carry you safely through the narrow path ahead.

Guidance by Life Area

Love

Timing and boundaries matter. Check whether the other person is ready before raising sensitive topics, and do not turn enthusiasm into pressure.

Check whether the other person is ready to discuss important issues:

  • Do not pressure them for an immediate answer.
  • Clarify sensitive boundaries in advance.

Career

Rules may be strict and mistakes costly. Confirm procedures, keep records around high-responsibility work, and do not overstep your authority.

Confirm rules and authority before important work:

  • Keep clear records when dealing with high-responsibility people.
  • Do not overreach in unfamiliar areas.

Health

When recovering or trying something new, start low and observe your response. If pain clearly worsens, step back instead of pushing through.

Start new activities at low intensity:

  • Check how your body responds the next day.
  • Step back if pain clearly worsens.

Finances

Stay within clear risk limits. Avoid leverage you cannot absorb and review terms before any major transaction.

Set clear limits for each investment and loss:

  • Do not use leverage you cannot afford.
  • Review terms before major transactions.

Relationships

With sensitive or powerful people, respectful communication matters. Be polite, state boundaries clearly, and avoid embarrassing someone publicly.

Think before raising sensitive topics:

  • Stay polite while stating clear boundaries.
  • Do not deliberately embarrass people in public.
